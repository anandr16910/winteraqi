# AuraGuard: Complete App Features & Capabilities

AuraGuard is an atmospheric intelligence and clean-air navigation web application engineered to protect families from winter smog, toxic particulate matter (PM2.5 / PM10), and ground-level thermal inversions.

---

## 1. Accurate Geolocation & Real-Time Monitoring
* **High-Precision Browser GPS Radar**: One-click "Use Exact Location" detects latitude and longitude within meter-level accuracy.
* **Sensor-Level Station Mapping**: Gemini with Google Search Grounding resolves the user's coordinates to the exact town/district and identifies the nearest official continuous ambient air monitoring station (e.g., US Embassy, CPCB, EPA, or municipal sensor).
* **Global City Search & Presets**: Instant search for any town or city worldwide, with curated 1-click presets for smog hotspot zones (New Delhi, Lahore, Beijing, Salt Lake City, Kraków, Sarajevo, Ulaanbaatar, Milan, Fresno, London).

## 2. Atmospheric & Thermal Inversion Diagnostics
* **Live Air Quality Index (US AQI)**: Dynamic color-coded gradient status card reflecting severity categories from *Good* to *Severe Hazardous Emergency*.
* **Particulate Concentration (PM2.5 & PM10)**: Real-time values in µg/m³ with comparative multipliers against WHO 24-hour health guidelines.
* **Inversion Risk Assessment**: Meteorological analysis of cold-air boundary lid traps where ground-level cooling prevents vertical dispersion.
* **Micro-Climate Humidity & Temperature Vectors**: Tracks temperature and relative humidity percentage, highlighting moisture conditions that accelerate particle agglomeration.
* **Dominant Smog Driver Profiling**: Real-time identification of dominant local sources (vehicular cold starts, solid fuel/biomass heating, agricultural residue, nitrate aerosols).
* **Live Grounded Web Citations**: Direct clickable citation badges to authoritative sources retrieved via Google Search Grounding.

## 3. Hyper-Local Micro-AQI & Clean Air Sanctuary Navigator
* **Micro-Climate Variance Engine**: Solves the reality that AQI is not uniform across a city. Highlights spots that are 30%–85% cleaner than street level.
* **6-Zone Micro-Location Comparison**:
  1. *Botanical Gardens & Parks*: Dense tree canopy particulate deposition (-25% to -45% PM2.5).
  2. *HVAC Filtered Public Sanctuaries*: Libraries, museums, and civic centers with commercial AHU filtration (-75% to -92% PM2.5).
  3. *High-Rise Elevation (12th–25th floors)*: Above nocturnal ground-inversion surface layers (-15% to -30% PM2.5).
  4. *Residential Pockets*: Interior cul-de-sacs set back from arterial traffic corridors (-10% to -20% PM2.5).
  5. *Street Canyons*: Trapped vehicular exhaust choke points (+20% to +45% dirtier).
  6. *Freight Depots & Transit Hubs*: Cold-start diesel vectors (+30% to +50% dirtier).
* **Interactive Custom Spot Micro-AQI Checker**: Allows typing any address, building name, school courtyard, or floor level to obtain an immediate aerodynamic micro-climate assessment and family safety advice.

## 4. Personalized Household & Vulnerability Profiling
* **Vulnerable Member Prioritization**: Custom protection toggles for infants/toddlers (2x respiratory rate per kg), elderly individuals (65+ cardiovascular risk), and asthmatic/bronchitis sufferers.
* **Pet Safety Protocol**: Specialized advice for domestic dogs and cats to avoid tracheal inflammation during inversion spikes.
* **Dwelling Architecture Adaptation**: Tailors recommendations for apartments, detached houses, or older drafty buildings with high infiltration rates (>1.5 ACH).
* **Commute & Heating Integration**: Personalizes advice based on public transit, two-wheelers, private vehicles, remote work, electric heat pumps, or solid fuel/gas boilers.

## 5. 24-Hour Diurnal Smog Routine Plan
* **Atmospheric Diurnal Synchronization**: Maps the day into 5 critical diurnal phases:
  - *Morning Inversion Peak (06:00 - 09:30 AM)*: Strict lockdown, workout deferral, sealed breathing zones.
  - *Solar Mixing Window (10:00 AM - 01:00 PM)*: Boundary layer ascent, safest essential errand window.
  - *Afternoon Low Trough (02:00 - 05:00 PM)*: Peak natural dispersion, filtered cross-ventilation.
  - *Evening Rush Hour Congestion (05:30 - 08:30 PM)*: Inversion collapse, entryway decontamination.
  - *Sleep Clean Sanctuary (09:00 PM - 06:00 AM)*: Low-volume bedroom filtration and nocturnal seals.
* **Dual-Action Directives**: Provides a *Family Protection Guideline* alongside a *Low-Emission Community Choice* to prevent adding pollution to the neighborhood.

## 6. Indoor Air Quality (IAQ) Lab & DIY Blueprints
* **Corsi-Rosenthal Box Blueprint**: Step-by-step instructions to build a MERV-13 box-fan purifier matching $600 commercial HEPA CADR for under $60.
* **Electrostatic Wet-Mopping Protocols**: Science-backed methods to prevent aerosolizing settled heavy metal soot compared to vacuuming.
* **Lidded Cooking & Thermal Moisture Traps**: Guidance to eliminate kitchen PM2.5 spikes (>500 µg/m³) caused by oil smoke and gas combustion.
* **Compression Weatherstrip & Gasket Engineering**: Low-cost drafts and doorway seals preventing outdoor infiltration.
* **Myth-Busting Insights**: Debunks common air cleaning misconceptions with peer-reviewed physics.

## 7. Direct Health & Community Impact Simulator
* **Interactive 24-Hour Exposure Curve**: Recharts visualization comparing outdoor street smog, unprotected indoor homes, and AuraGuard sealed sanctuaries.
* **Inhaled PM2.5 Avoided (µg/day)**: Real-time calculation of deep alveolar particulate saved per person.
* **Local CO2e Emissions Prevented (kg)**: Quantifies community carbon reductions achieved via carpooling, anti-idling, and clean heating adjustments.
* **Acute Respiratory Symptom Reduction Percentage**: Predicts asthma flare-up, coughing, and ocular irritation mitigation.
* **Community Clean-Air Multiplier**: Visualizes how neighborhood adoption scales air quality improvements.

## 8. AI Smog Doctor & Conversational Assistant
* **Gemini 2.5 Flash Engine**: Real-time interactive consultation with search grounding.
* **Multi-Turn Context Awareness**: Remembers location AQI, weather conditions, family medical sensitivities, and dwelling type.
* **One-Click Quick Prompts**: Instant answers on infant safety, N95 seal verification, houseplant air filtration realities, and low-smoke cooking oils.
* **Verifiable Grounding Citations**: Displays clickable research citations directly in conversational bubbles.
